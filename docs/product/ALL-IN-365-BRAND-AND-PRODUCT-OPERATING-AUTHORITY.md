# ALL IN 365 — Brand and Product Operating Authority

This is a V2-owned working consolidation of durable V1 brand and product decisions. It is not a replacement for newer explicit founder decisions or feature-specific acceptance contracts.

## Brand identity
- Product name: **ALL IN 365**.
- Philosophy: **Live All In.**
- Positioning: **Your whole fitness life in one place.**
- In-app AI identity: **Coach**.
- Social destination: **Community**.
- Race-centered community: **RaceHub**.
- Do not abbreviate the product to AI365.
- Avoid generic “elite”, “beast mode”, “no excuses” and similar hype language.

## Product principles
- Serve people starting from no exercise through endurance, multisport, strength and other athletic goals.
- “Start where you are, go as far as you want.”
- Whole-fitness-life product: training, nutrition, recovery and community work together, but the interface stays simple and context-aware.
- Coach is one persistent assistant/conversation, not multiple personas or parallel chat systems.
- Coach may prepare reviewed actions; canonical mutations require explicit user intent/approval and the owning domain writer.
- Manual controls remain first-class.
- Community posting is explicit; imported history or Coach does not autonomously publish.
- Product simplicity / anti-bloat rule: add a major user-facing feature only when it replaces an app/service users otherwise need or materially simplifies/strengthens an existing workflow. Otherwise improve speed, clarity, reliability, progressive disclosure and discoverability.

## Business / launch decisions requiring current reconciliation
- Legal operator: Durben Ventures LLC.
- Current founder pricing decision: $9.99/month, with a seven-day free trial.
- No free tier and no advertisements.
- Annual price has appeared as a $50 placeholder in prior planning; do not treat it as approved for launch without a fresh decision.
- Referral milestone structure: 5 / 10 / 25; reward definitions beyond the approved five-referral apparel choice require current confirmation.
- Target average AI spend below $1 per active user per month; preserve rate limits, cost controls and safety.
- Beta should begin with a small, controlled founder cohort; public launch remains gated by product, legal, security, provider and device acceptance.

## Founder-approved V2 product-policy principles — 2026-11-12

**Authority:** [ALL-219 founder approval](https://linear.app/allin365/issue/ALL-219/v1-v2-legal-business-plan-and-brand-authority-transfer), dated 2026-11-12. **Status:** APPROVED as durable product/implementation principles **only**; not an approval of exact legal clauses or release.

1. **Privacy first:** Health information and progress photographs stay private by default. Sharing publicly requires a separate user-directed action.
2. **User-controlled data:** People may request account-data exports and deletion. Do not advertise fulfillment until the full data lifecycle, processing, scope, security, retention and validation actually work.
3. **Transparent Coach processing:** Coach may use only appropriately authorized and relevant information. Identify actual AI processors, fields shared, and data handling accurately in user-facing disclosures before enabling those flows.
4. **Beta and paid billing are separate:** Founder-beta participation is not payment authorization. Trial enrollment and any paid renewal require a distinct valid purchase flow and applicable disclosures.
5. **Adults-only founder beta:** Confirm age 18+ and separately record review/acceptance of the current Terms, Privacy Policy and Founder Beta Terms using the existing canonical versioned legal-acceptance mechanism.

**Release boundary:** This founder decision does **not** approve legal wording, attorney/privacy review, legal-document publication, account-deletion executor activation, production deployment, opening signup or payment collection. Do not treat the ALL-219 principle approval as the founder signature on final legal text; that requires a later explicit exact-version decision.
