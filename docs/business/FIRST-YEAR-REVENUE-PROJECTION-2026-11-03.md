## Assumptions in the source projection

- ALL IN 365 fitness app under Durben Ventures LLC.
- The projection used $9.99 per paid month following a seven-day free trial.
- First two operating months assumed no paid subscribers, then gradual growth to 200 subscriber equivalents in month 12.
- Subscriber-month counts already reflect projected cancellations.
- Merchandise, annual plans and other possible revenue sources were excluded.
- No operating expenses, processing fees, taxes, refunds or marketing costs were deducted. This is gross sales, not net income.
