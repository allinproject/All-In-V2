## Privacy Policy

**Version:**`privacy-policy-v2-candidate`

ALL IN 365 is a fitness, training, nutrition, recovery, and wellness platform designed to bring a user's fitness life together in one place.

This Privacy Policy explains how ALL IN 365 ("ALL IN 365," "we," "us," or "our"), operated by **Durben Ventures LLC**, collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you use the ALL IN 365 mobile application, website, services, Coach features, Community, RaceHub, and related features.

Our goal is to collect and use information only where it serves the ALL IN 365 experience.

### Information We Collect

Depending on the features you choose to use, we may collect account and profile information; training and activity information; health and fitness information you authorize through Apple Health or another supported service; nutrition and fueling information; recovery and wellness information; progress photos and other images; Community and RaceHub content; Coach interactions; device, app, diagnostic, security, and technical information; and feedback/support communications.

#### Progress Photos

You may choose to upload photographs, including private progress photographs.

**Progress photos are private by default.**

A private progress photo will not be posted to Community merely because it was uploaded to ALL IN 365. Sharing requires a separate user-directed action where sharing functionality is available.

#### Apple Health / HealthKit

If you choose to connect Apple Health, ALL IN 365 may access only the health and fitness information you specifically authorize. Depending on permissions, this may include workout/activity information, heart rate, steps, distance, energy expenditure, weight/body measurements, sleep information, and other supported health and fitness information relevant to features you choose to use.

ALL IN 365 will not sell HealthKit data, use HealthKit data for third-party advertising, use HealthKit data for unrelated marketing, share HealthKit data with data brokers, or use HealthKit data for unrelated profiling.

#### Coach and AI

When Coach's AI features are available and you choose to use them, messages you submit and relevant account, training, nutrition, recovery, goal, and preference information may be processed by an AI service provider to generate a response. ALL IN 365 intends to use OpenAI’s API for Coach; its exact V2 integration and the categories of data transmitted must be verified before Coach is enabled. [OpenAI’s API data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data) describe how OpenAI handles API data.

We do not authorize AI service providers to use ALL IN 365 health information for advertising.

### How We Use Information

We may use personal information to create and maintain accounts; import and display authorized fitness information; provide training, nutrition, fueling, recovery, progress, equipment, RaceHub, Community, Meal Prep, pantry, and Coach features; provide user-requested calculations and recommendations; operate and secure the service; diagnose bugs and crashes; improve usability; prevent fraud/abuse; respond to support; meet legal obligations; and protect users and ALL IN 365.

We do not sell personal information.

We do not use personal information for third-party targeted advertising.

We do not use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or unrelated data mining.

### Sensitive Information

Training, nutrition, body measurements, health information, progress photographs, and similar information may be sensitive. We use sensitive information only for purposes reasonably necessary to provide or improve requested functionality, operate and secure the service, comply with law, or otherwise with appropriate consent.

### Sharing

We may share personal information with service providers that perform functions on our behalf, such as cloud hosting, database infrastructure, authentication, AI processing, error monitoring, analytics used to operate/improve ALL IN 365, email/communications, security, and customer support.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law/legal process; protect safety or rights; investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents, or Terms violations; or protect ALL IN 365 and its property.

If ALL IN 365 or its operating entity is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction subject to applicable law.

### What We Do Not Sell or Use for Targeted Advertising

ALL IN 365 does not currently sell personal data, health or fitness data, or progress photographs; use HealthKit information for advertising; operate third-party behavioral advertising within the app; or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

If these practices materially change, the Privacy Policy and legally required consent mechanisms must be updated before the new practice begins.

### Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing the service, maintaining account history requested by the user, securing the service, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, and meeting legal obligations.

Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

When an account is deleted, we will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify personal information associated with the account unless retention is required or permitted for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, backup-integrity, dispute-resolution, or similar purposes.

Our infrastructure may maintain backups or other recovery copies as part of its service. The availability, frequency, retention, and contents of any such copies depend on the actual infrastructure configuration. We do not promise a particular backup schedule or recovery period in this founder-beta policy. Some information may persist temporarily in backup or security records where appropriate and permitted by law.

Aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably be linked to an individual may be retained for product improvement, analytics, research, and other lawful purposes.

### Account Deletion

The V2 application currently offers signed-in users an in-app way to submit an account-deletion request, view its status, and cancel the request while it is still pending. **Submitting a request does not immediately delete your account or personal information.** The secure deletion processor is not yet enabled, and permanent deletion has not been validated as a completed service. For privacy or deletion questions you may contact **durbenventures@gmail.com**; requests may require identity verification and will be handled subject to applicable law. This description of current request intake is not a representation that the complete account-deletion lifecycle is ready for release.

Account deletion is intended to delete the user's ALL IN 365 account and associated personal information that we are not legally required or otherwise permitted to retain.

Deleting ALL IN 365 does not automatically delete information maintained independently by Apple Health or another external service.

### Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to confirm processing, access, correct, delete, obtain a portable copy of certain information, withdraw consent where processing depends on consent, appeal certain privacy decisions, and opt out of certain processing where applicable.

ALL IN 365 currently does not sell personal data or process personal data for third-party targeted advertising.

Privacy requests: **durbenventures@gmail.com**

Minnesota residents may have additional rights under the Minnesota Consumer Data Privacy Act where applicable, including access, correction, deletion, portability, information about processing, and appeal rights.

Request/appeal contact: **durbenventures@gmail.com**

### Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect personal information. No internet service, mobile application, database, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

### Health-Information Breaches

Certain health applications may be subject to the Federal Trade Commission Health Breach Notification Rule or other breach-notification laws. If ALL IN 365 experiences a breach for which notification is legally required, required notices will be provided.

### Children

The founder beta is intended only for individuals at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly permit children under 13 to create founder-beta accounts. Any future availability to minors requires appropriate safeguards, disclosures, consent mechanisms, and policy updates.

### Third-Party Services

Third-party services have their own privacy practices, terms, and controls. Information maintained by a third-party service is governed by that service's policies when outside ALL IN 365's control.

### Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as ALL IN 365 develops. Material changes affecting personal-information use will receive notice appropriate to the significance of the change and consent where required by law.
